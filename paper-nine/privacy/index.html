Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Paper Nine requires no account. Sudoku puzzles, answers, progress, and pencil notes are stored locally on your device and are not sent to JimmyJanson.

1. Information stored on your device

Paper Nine stores your puzzle entries, pencil notes, completed pages, active Puzzle Book, and preferences locally so that you can continue playing offline. JimmyJanson does not operate a server that receives this information. You can remove it by resetting a Puzzle Book in the app or by deleting the app.

2. Advertising

Paper Nine may use Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising. Where required, Paper Nine asks for your privacy choices before requesting ads. Available privacy options can be changed from within the app.

Google’s handling of information is governed by its Privacy Policy and information about partner services.

3. Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the app. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or to meet legal obligations.

4. Children

Paper Nine is a general-audience puzzle app and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support message, contact us so we can delete it.

5. Your choices and rights

You can remove locally stored game data by resetting it or deleting the app. Where an advertising privacy-options entry point is required, it is available in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding personal information. Contact us to make a request.

6. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. Advertising providers may process information in countries other than your own subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when Paper Nine’s features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.
