Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Paper Nine requires no account and does not collect, transmit, or share personal data through the app. Puzzle progress, entries, pencil notes, custom handwriting, and preferences remain on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Paper Nine stores your puzzle entries, pencil notes, completed pages, active Puzzle Book, custom handwritten digits, and preferences locally so that you can continue playing offline. JimmyJanson does not operate a server that receives this information. You can remove locally stored data by using the app’s reset and deletion features or by deleting the app.

2. Data collection and sharing

Paper Nine does not collect or share personal information, device identifiers, precise or approximate location, usage data, diagnostics, or advertising data. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

3. Support messages

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include. This communication occurs through your email provider rather than through the app. We use the information only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the app, and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or to meet legal obligations.

4. Children

Paper Nine is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed specifically to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support message, contact us to request its deletion.

5. Your choices

You can remove game data stored on your device by resetting it within the app or deleting the app. You may request deletion of information that you voluntarily sent in a support email by contacting us.

6. Security

Because Paper Nine does not transmit game data to JimmyJanson, that information remains under the protections provided by your device and operating system. We take reasonable measures to protect any support correspondence under our control.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.
